my story

a lifetime of martial arts

I've been a martial artist for most of my life. Showing up when I'm tired, drilling the basics until they're automatic, and learning from every loss are the same habits I bring to debugging a circuit or finishing a long project. These days I train BJJ and coach high school wrestling, which taught me that explaining something clearly matters just as much as knowing it.
